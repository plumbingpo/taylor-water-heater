Safety

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Taylor Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A commercial water heater failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling fast for emergency repairs throughout Taylor and the surrounding area, and planned work can be booked after hours to limit downtime.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless bank throwing error codes. Whatever the cause, we diagnose it fast, get hot water flowing again, and record every step for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Ongoing Protection

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

Every Taylor Water Heater program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve tests, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.
